Who am I?

I am an Embedded Software Engineer with experience in automotive ECUs, autonomous drones, residential automation, and high-reliability embedded systems. My work focuses on firmware development, debugging, optimization, and integration of embedded technologies.

I have practical experience with C, C++, and Python, as well as microcontrollers such as STM32, ESP32, ESP8266, and ATmega. My background also includes working with communication protocols such as CAN, SPI, I2C, LoRa, WiFi, and Bluetooth.

Throughout my career, I have contributed to software for combustion engine ECUs, autonomous spraying drones, and smart automation systems. My work often involves sensor integration, telemetry, data analysis, and developing optimized solutions for real-time embedded applications.

I am motivated by solving complex engineering problems and building efficient, reliable, and maintainable embedded systems. I am constantly seeking new challenges and opportunities to advance in the fields of automotive software, embedded control, and system integration.
